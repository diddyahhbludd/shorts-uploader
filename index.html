Shorts Uploader

Shorts Uploader is a personal tool that uploads short videos made by its owner to the owner's own YouTube channel, using the YouTube Data API.

  • It is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer.
  • It requests permission to upload videos and to read the name of the signed-in channel (to confirm where uploads go).
  • It does not collect, sell or share anyone's data.
